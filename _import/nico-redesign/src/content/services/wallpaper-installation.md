Wallpaper has made a massive comeback in interior design, offering textures, patterns, and depth that paint simply can't match. But hanging modern, high-end wallpaper is an exacting craft. A misaligned pattern, a visible seam, or trapped air bubbles will completely ruin the look of an expensive roll of paper.

Go Pro Home Improvements provides professional wallpaper installation and removal across Orlando and Central Florida. We know that the secret to flawless wallpaper is in the wall preparation. We take the time to repair any dings, sand the walls smooth, and apply the correct sizing primer before the first drop of paper goes up.
