Adding crown molding, tall baseboards, or custom wainscoting is one of the highest-ROI upgrades you can make to a home's interior. It instantly adds architectural character and makes any room feel finished and expensive. But trim carpentry is incredibly unforgiving—a bad miter cut or a sloppy caulking job stands out immediately.

Go Pro Home Improvements brings 15 years of carpentry and painting expertise to every trim project in Central Florida. We don't just cut corners at 45 degrees and fill the gaps with caulk. We use proper coping techniques for inside corners, ensuring that your molding stays tight and beautiful even as the house shifts in the summer humidity.
