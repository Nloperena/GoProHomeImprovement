Before you can build the kitchen or bathroom of your dreams, you have to tear out the old one. But swinging a sledgehammer blindly is a great way to burst a hidden pipe, cut a live electrical wire, or blanket your entire house in toxic drywall dust. Professional interior demolition requires surgical precision, not just brute force.

Go Pro Home Improvements provides safe, selective interior demolition services for homeowners and remodeling contractors across Central Florida. With 15 years of experience, we know how homes in Orlando and Kissimmee are built. We know where the plumbing vents hide and how to carefully peel away old tile without destroying the subfloor.
